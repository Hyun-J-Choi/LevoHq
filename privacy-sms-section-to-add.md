## 4. SMS Communications and Mobile Information

LevoHQ, operated by LQ Capital Holdings LLC, provides SMS messaging services on behalf of Medspa Clients. The following terms apply to all SMS communications sent through the LevoHQ platform.

**Consent.** End Users (patients of a Medspa Client) provide affirmative, TCPA-compliant prior express written consent before any SMS is sent. Consent is collected via a non-pre-checked opt-in checkbox on the Medspa Client's intake form. The exact disclosure language and sample consent form are publicly viewable at https://levohq.ai/sms-consent. Consent is logged with timestamp, source URL, and the exact disclosure shown.

**Types of messages.** SMS messages sent through LevoHQ include appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and replies to patient inquiries (such as questions about hours, services, pricing, and bookings). Messages are sent by an automated AI-powered assistant operated by LevoHQ on behalf of the Medspa Client.

**Frequency.** Message frequency varies by Medspa Client and patient activity. A typical patient receives approximately 2–6 messages per month.

**Rates.** Standard message and data rates may apply. LevoHQ does not charge End Users directly; carrier rates from the End User's mobile provider may apply.

**Opt-out.** End Users may opt out at any time by replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REVOKE, or STOPALL. Opt-out is processed immediately and no further messages are sent unless the End User provides renewed affirmative consent.

**Help.** End Users may reply HELP or INFO at any time for assistance and contact information.

**No sharing of mobile data for marketing.** LevoHQ does not share End User mobile phone numbers, opt-in status, or SMS content with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information collected for the purpose of providing SMS communications is used solely to deliver the Service to the End User on behalf of the Medspa Client.

**Consent is not a condition of purchase.** Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of receiving services from any Medspa Client.
